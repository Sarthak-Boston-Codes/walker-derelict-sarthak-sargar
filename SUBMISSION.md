Summary of my changes: For Assignment 3, I added three 3D assets to DERELICT without touching the existing 2D gameplay — a supply crate, a rusted barrel, and a medkit pickup. Each one was specified first in CHANGE-BRIEF.md (exact size, origin point, look, and triangle budget) before any building started. I used the Blender MCP to have Claude build each asset directly inside my open Blender session, one object at a time, and each has its own .blend file saved in tools/blender/. Partway through I caught a real bug: the brief's colors were being read as linear values instead of sRGB, which made everything look washed out, so I had that fixed with a proper conversion rather than just eyeballing it. Where the brief didn't specify exact colors or proportions, I reviewed and approved what Claude picked. All three assets export to godot/assets/3d/ and sit together in a new showcase scene (godot/scenes/showcase_3d.tscn) with a floor, one light, and one camera. I wrote an automated test that checks every asset against the brief's predicted failure cases, plus size and triangle count. My real inspect-and-revise moment was the medkit's cross — it was too small to see at the planned camera angle, and fixing it took both enlarging the cross and changing the camera angle, not just one or the other. I also did a hand-edit in Blender myself (resizing the medkit), logged exactly what it changed, and reverted it — the film shows the automated test catching that same kind of change if it had been exported.
Known limitations: (detail in TEST-REPORT.md's Assignment 3 section, CHANGE-BRIEF.md Revisions, and the film's FACTCHECK.md) The assets are built from overlapping boxes rather than one merged surface, so some faces are hidden inside the crate and medkit (counted in their triangle totals). The crate's strap and body bottoms share a plane and may flicker when seen from below. The barrel is flat-shaded, so its 12 facets show. The medkit's cross is only 1 cm tall, so it's a thin sliver from front and side views. With one light and no ambient fill, shadowed sides go very dark, and shadows read as slightly small. The floor is a single-sided plane. The test's printed SCALE column shows only each asset's top-level node; it checks the mesh nodes too, but a bad mesh scale shows up in the SIZE column. The 3D assets aren't connected to the 2D game yet.
