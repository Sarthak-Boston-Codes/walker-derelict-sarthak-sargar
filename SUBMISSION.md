Summary of my changes: I added three 3D assets to DERELICT without touching its 2D gameplay: a supply crate, a rusted barrel and a medkit, each specified in CHANGE-BRIEF.md (size, origin, look, triangle budget) before anything was built. Claude built them in Blender through the Blender MCP, one object per call, and each has its own .blend source in tools/blender/. Along the way I corrected the colour handling — the brief's colours are sRGB picker values, so they're converted to linear before going into Blender's shader — and approved the colours and proportions Claude picked where the brief gave no numbers. The assets are exported to godot/assets/3d/ and shown in a separate showcase scene (godot/scenes/showcase_3d.tscn) with a floor, one light and one camera. An automated test (godot/tests/showcase_3d_test.gd) checks each asset against the brief's three predicted failure cases plus size and triangle budget. My inspect-and-revise cycle was the medkit's cross: it didn't read at the planned camera angle, and it took both a bigger cross and a steeper camera to fix. I also tried a hand edit in Blender, logged and reverted it, and the film shows the test catching that kind of edit if it were exported.
Known limitations: (detail in TEST-REPORT.md's Assignment 3 section, CHANGE-BRIEF.md Revisions, and the film's FACTCHECK.md) The assets are built from overlapping boxes rather than one merged surface, so some faces are hidden inside the crate and medkit (counted in their triangle totals). The crate's strap and body bottoms share a plane and may flicker when seen from below. The barrel is flat-shaded, so its 12 facets show. The medkit's cross is only 1 cm tall, so it's a thin sliver from front and side views. With one light and no ambient fill, shadowed sides go very dark, and shadows read as slightly small. The floor is a single-sided plane. The test's printed SCALE column shows only each asset's top-level node; it checks the mesh nodes too, but a bad mesh scale shows up in the SIZE column. The 3D assets aren't connected to the 2D game yet.
