# STORYBOARD.md — DERELICT

**Sketches: `design/storyboard/sheet.svg`** — one combined sheet, all 7 panels, numbered to match below. (Made for you given the time crunch — open it, and if you want to change anything, just say what.)

Frame shape: 16:9 throughout. Gameplay panels show the actual in-game camera (top-down / bird's-eye, fixed). Design panels are mood/intro shots outside normal play and are labeled as such.

Shot types used: **wide, medium, close-up**. Angles used: **high/aerial** (panel 1), **eye level** (panel 2), **bird's-eye / top-down**, the gameplay camera (panels 3–7) — three distinct angles.

---

## Panel 1 — Title / intro (first thing the player sees)
- Shot: wide · high angle · **design view**
- Player action: none — title card before play begins
- See: game title over a dim, derelict interior silhouette
- Hear: wind, a single distant groan, music fading in
- Assets: (mood only — no in-game asset required)
- Design reason (pillar "the world was abandoned for a reason"): sets tone before play starts

## Panel 2 — Character intro
- Shot: close-up · eye level · **design view**
- Player action: none — checking the weapon before moving on
- See: CHAR-IDLE detail — gear, flashlight mount, weapon low-ready
- Hear: a quiet mechanical click, music
- Assets: CHAR-IDLE
- Design reason: establishes who the player is controlling — cautious, not a soldier

## Panel 3 — Core action: encounter
- Shot: medium · bird's-eye (gameplay camera) · **gameplay view**
- Player action: spots ZOMBIE-SHAMBLE, raises weapon (CHAR-AIM)
- See: CHAR-AIM, ZOMBIE-SHAMBLE approaching, flashlight fixed on it
- Hear: music drops out almost entirely, footsteps, the infected's groan
- Assets: CHAR-AIM, ZOMBIE-SHAMBLE
- Design reason (pillar "violence is a last resort"): the aim pose is a visible beat before anything fires

## Panel 4 — Success
- Shot: close-up (gameplay camera pushed in) · bird's-eye · **gameplay view**
- Player action: fires; the infected goes down
- See: CHAR-SHOOT-FOLLOWTHROUGH, ZOMBIE-DOWN
- Hear: SFX-SHOT, then SFX-DOWN
- Assets: CHAR-SHOOT-FOLLOWTHROUGH, ZOMBIE-DOWN, SFX-SHOT, SFX-DOWN
- Design reason: the infected visibly takes the hit before going down — no instant disappearance

## Panel 5 — Failure
- Shot: medium · bird's-eye · **gameplay view**
- Player action: a second infected grabs the player from a blind corner
- See: CHAR-GRABBED-FAIL, the attacker clearly on the player's flank
- Hear: SFX-HURT
- Assets: CHAR-GRABBED-FAIL, SFX-HURT
- Design reason (pillar "every failure teaches a route"): the attacker's position is unambiguous

## Panel 6 — Recovery
- Shot: medium · bird's-eye · **gameplay view**
- Player action: breaks free, re-arms, continues
- See: CHAR-RECOVER, then back to CHAR-IDLE/CHAR-WALK
- Hear: music returning quietly
- Assets: CHAR-RECOVER
- Design reason: control returns cleanly after a hit — the retry is immediate, not punishing

## Panel 7 — End of session
- Shot: wide · bird's-eye · **gameplay view**
- Player action: reaches the extraction marker
- See: CHAR-CELEBRATE, the exit clearly lit
- Hear: SFX-CLEAR, music fading out
- Assets: CHAR-CELEBRATE, SFX-CLEAR
- Design reason: a clear, calm signal the session ended successfully, readable even muted
