## Assignment 7 — keycard spin sheet

- **`godot/art/keycard_sheet.png`**: generated by code, `tools/make_keycard_sheet.py` (Pillow), written and run by Claude Code on **2026-11-20**. No third-party art, font or licence: every shape is drawn by the script. The committed PNG is the script's output, unedited (SHA-256 `df945a6c26686670f80150551e21aae31dc5e2481b8b0f76159a4f4b2cca715f`); its `.import` file is Godot 4.7.2's default.
- **Cell size:** 64 × 64 px. **Grid:** 8 × 1 (`hframes` 8, `vframes` 1), sheet 512 × 64, `frame` 0–7.
- **Frame order:** left to right, cell k = the card turned k × 45° about its vertical axis (0°, 45°, …, 315°); drawn width |cos θ| × 52 px. Cells 2 and 6 are edge-on (a 2 px line). Front face (dark navy, cyan rim, gold chip) on cells 7, 0, 1; back face (orange, black magnetic stripe) on cells 3, 4, 5 (see CHANGE-BRIEF.md revision 2026-11-20, sheet). No 360° cell.
- **Registration:** every cell's bounding box is centred on x = 32, y = 32 (script output: cells 0/4 x 6–58, cells 1/3/5/7 x 13–51, cells 2/6 x 31–33).
